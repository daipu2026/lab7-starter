# Places in Japan (日本の観光地)

## Chuson-ji (中尊寺) — Hiraizumi, Iwate (岩手県平泉町)
- Category: Buddhist temple / 寺, UNESCO World Heritage site
- Highlights: Konjikido golden hall (金色堂) and historic temple buildings
- Access: JR Tohoku Line to Hiraizumi Station; Chusonji is about 1.6 km from the station
- Cost: Regular admission for the Konjikido and related buildings is listed as 1,000 yen for adults
- Best season: Autumn leaves; Hiraizumi is highlighted as a regional foliage destination
- Source: https://www.chusonji.or.jp/ and https://hiraizumi.or.jp/info/access.html (checked 2026-11)

## Fushimi Inari Taisha (伏見稲荷大社) — Kyoto, Kyoto (京都府京都市)
- Category: Shinto shrine / 神社, head shrine of Inari worship
- Highlights: Thousands of vermilion torii gates; mountain shrine paths
- Access: JR Nara Line to Inari Station, next to the shrine; Keihan Fushimi-inari Station is about a five-minute walk
- Cost: The official access page lists no admission price; verify any special event costs (estimate)
- Best season: Year-round; early morning is a quieter time to walk
- Source: https://inari.jp/en/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Kiyomizu-dera (清水寺) — Kyoto, Kyoto (京都府京都市)
- Category: Buddhist temple / 寺, historic hillside temple
- Highlights: Kannon worship, the main hall, and views over eastern Kyoto
- Access: From Kyoto Station, take city bus 206 or 100 to Gojozaka, then walk uphill about 10 minutes
- Cost: Admission price can change; check the official visitor guide (estimate)
- Best season: Autumn leaves (紅葉); check the official visitor guide for seasonal night-viewing dates
- Source: https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/en/location/ (checked 2026-11)

## Kinkaku-ji (金閣寺) — Kyoto, Kyoto (京都府京都市)
- Category: Zen Buddhist temple / 禅寺, Rokuon-ji
- Highlights: The Golden Pavilion reflected in its garden pond
- Access: Kyoto City Bus to Kinkakuji-michi; follow the official temple access guidance
- Cost: Adult admission is listed as 500 yen on the official site; verify before visiting
- Best season: Year-round; the garden scenery changes with the seasons
- Source: https://www.shokoku-ji.jp/kinkakuji/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Kenrokuen Garden (兼六園) — Kanazawa, Ishikawa (石川県金沢市)
- Category: Japanese landscape garden / 日本庭園, designated scenic garden
- Highlights: Pond, bridges, seasonal flowers, and the yukitsuri winter ropes
- Access: From Kanazawa Station, bus to Kenrokuen-shita/Kanazawa Castle, then walk about five minutes
- Cost: Garden admission price is not listed here; check the official visitor information (estimate)
- Best season: Each season has a different garden view; check official updates for bloom periods
- Source: https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/access.html and https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/season.html (checked 2026-11)

## Nara Park (奈良公園) — Nara, Nara (奈良県奈良市)
- Category: Public park / 公園, deer and cultural heritage area
- Highlights: Free-roaming deer and walking routes among temples and museums
- Access: Walk east from Kintetsu-Nara Station; JR Nara Station is also reachable on foot or by bus
- Cost: Park grounds are open to walk through; individual attractions and deer crackers may cost extra
- Best season: All seasons; spring greenery and autumn leaves are popular
- Source: https://www.visitnara.jp/destinations/area/nara-park/ (checked 2026-11)

## Himeji Castle (姫路城) — Himeji, Hyogo (兵庫県姫路市)
- Category: Japanese castle / 城, UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Highlights: White exterior, preserved keep, and historic defensive layout
- Access: Walk about 20 minutes from JR Himeji Station, or take a bus to Otemon-mae and walk about five minutes
- Cost: Admission prices may change; check the official city guide before booking (estimate)
- Best season: Year-round; check official seasonal events and opening information before visiting
- Source: https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/castle/0000007671.html and https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/castle/ (checked 2026-11)

## Itsukushima Shrine (厳島神社) — Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima (広島県廿日市市)
- Category: Shinto shrine / 神社, UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Highlights: Shrine buildings and the celebrated torii gate beside the sea
- Access: On Miyajima Island; check the shrine's official site for current island and visitor access details
- Cost: Admission and any crossing costs can change; confirm current prices before travel (estimate)
- Best season: Year-round; check local tide information when planning a close view of the sea gate
- Source: https://www.itsukushimajinja.jp/en/ and https://www.itsukushimajinja.jp/ (checked 2026-11)

## Mount Fuji (富士山) — Fujinomiya, Shizuoka (静岡県富士宮市)
- Category: Volcano / 火山, UNESCO World Heritage cultural property
- Highlights: Summit views, crater rim, and several established climbing trails
- Access: Use an official trail and its designated fifth or sixth station; check current route and seasonal closures
- Cost: Climbing fees and transport depend on route and season; verify with the official climbing site (estimate)
- Best season: Official summer climbing season only; off-season climbing is dangerous
- Source: https://www.fujisan-climb.jp/en/ and https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1418/ (checked 2026-11)

## Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum (広島平和記念資料館) — Hiroshima, Hiroshima (広島県広島市)
- Category: History museum / 歴史資料館, peace education
- Highlights: Exhibits documenting the atomic bombing and its human impact
- Access: In Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park; check the museum's official site for current visitor guidance
- Cost: Ticket and timed-entry conditions can change; check the official visitor page (estimate)
- Best season: Year-round; allow time for the museum and surrounding memorial park
- Source: https://hpmmuseum.jp/?lang=eng (checked 2026-11)

## Matsushima Bay (松島湾) — Matsushima, Miyagi (宮城県松島町)
- Category: Coastal landscape / 海岸景勝地, one of Japan's celebrated scenic views
- Highlights: Pine-covered islands, viewpoints, boat trips, and local seafood
- Access: JR Senseki Line from Sendai to Matsushimakaigan Station takes about 40 minutes
- Cost: Viewpoints, boats, and individual attractions have separate costs (estimate)
- Best season: Year-round; oysters are a winter specialty and the scenery changes by season
- Source: https://www.matsushima-kanko.com/en/ (checked 2026-11)

## Zuiganji Temple (瑞巌寺) — Matsushima, Miyagi (宮城県松島町)
- Category: Zen Buddhist temple / 禅寺, historic cultural site
- Highlights: A historic temple associated with the Date Masamune era and Matsushima's cultural landscape
- Access: In Matsushima; JR Senseki Line serves Matsushimakaigan Station for the waterfront area
- Cost: Temple admission is separate; confirm the current price with the official temple before travel (estimate)
- Best season: Autumn and the full-moon season, when the Matsushima area holds related events
- Source: https://www.matsushima-kanko.com/en/ (checked 2026-11)

## Hirosaki Castle (弘前城) — Hirosaki, Aomori (青森県弘前市)
- Category: Japanese castle / 城, castle park and seasonal event site
- Highlights: Cherry blossoms (桜) and autumn foliage (紅葉) in Hirosaki Park (弘前公園), plus the Hirosaki Neputa summer festival
- Access: In Hirosaki Park on the former castle grounds; check local visitor information for current transit
- Cost: Park and castle-area charges vary by season and event; check current official visitor information (estimate)
- Best season: Late April to early May for blossoms; mid-October to mid-November for park foliage
- Source: https://www.hirosaki-kanko.or.jp/en/edit.html?id=edit01 (checked 2026-11)
